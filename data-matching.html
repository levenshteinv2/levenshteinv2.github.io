Applications

Data Matching and Entity Resolution with Edit Distance

This section examines how Levenshtein distance and related string-similarity methods contribute to record linkage and entity resolution, including noisy names and addresses, geographic records, patient identity, and privacy-preserving matching, while distinguishing textual similarity from evidence that two records represent the same real-world entity.

  1. 01

    Privacy-Preserving Record Linkage: Bloom Filters, PSI, Secure Computation & Attacks

    A rigorous guide to privacy-preserving record linkage: Bloom filters, q-gram encoding, cryptanalysis, private set intersection, secure multiparty computation, blocking, privacy leakage, and linkage evaluation.

    Article·43 min read

  2. 02

    Patient Identity Matching: Algorithms, MPI, Safety & Record Linkage

    A rigorous guide to patient identity matching: MPI and EMPI design, demographic comparators, probabilistic and referential matching, duplicate and overlay detection, FHIR $match, evaluation, safety, and privacy.

    Article·40 min read

  3. 03

    Name, Address & Place Matching: Normalization, Similarity & Entity Resolution

    A rigorous guide to matching names, addresses and places using edit distance, Jaro-Winkler, phonetic methods, structured parsing, transliteration, geography, blocking, and probabilistic evidence.

    Article·32 min read

  4. 04

    Entity Resolution & Record Linkage with Edit Distance: Models, Blocking & Decision Rules

    A rigorous guide to entity resolution and record linkage: Fellegi-Sunter matching, edit-distance comparators, blocking, probabilistic evidence, clustering, evaluation, and failure modes.

    Article·28 min read
